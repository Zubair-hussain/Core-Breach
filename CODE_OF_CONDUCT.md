# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful.

- Treat everyone with respect, whatever their experience level, background or country.
- Give feedback on the code and the ideas, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and say so when something is unclear instead of guessing.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or spam.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who repeat the behaviour.

To report a problem, open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/Zubair-hussain/Core-Breach/security/advisories/new) or contact the maintainer through [zubairhussain.xyz](https://zubairhussain.xyz).
